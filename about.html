Club5to7 began in a cramped flat off Bole Road in late 2023, with three friends, a borrowed projector, and a print of Sembène's Black Girl. We sat on cushions. We argued politely. We did it again the next month.

We watch one film a month, together, between five and seven in the evening — a softer hour, when the city is loud but distracted. We pick a theme. We pick a film inside the theme. We discuss afterwards over coffee from a round pot.

We are unfussy and unaffiliated. There are no critics here, only people who like the dark. Membership is by introduction. The digest is by us, for us. The folding chairs are limited.

— The Editors

What we believe (kind of)

Three half-rules
— 01

Watch slowly, talk briefly

We don't review films, we have arguments about them. The film is the long part. The argument is the short part. The coffee is the longest part.

— 02

African cinema first, always

Not exclusively. But by default. Sembène, Gerima, Mambéty, Mehari — these are our cornerstones. Everything else is a comparison.

— 03

The room is small on purpose

Eighteen chairs. That's the cap. When the room outgrows it, we split into two, not bigger. Intimacy is the format.
